The problem

Finding a good place to go is messy.

Reviews are scattered

Quality signals live across dozens of apps — hard to compare and easy to fake.

Little trust or moderation

No way to vet helpfulness, and toxic or misleading reviews go unchecked.

Planning takes too long

Shortlists, maps and bookings live in different tabs — nothing is saved for later.

Jaatra's answer

One shared home for travel discovery.

Discover hotels, restaurants and attractions by category, city or keyword. Rate and review them, save a wishlist, flag bad content — all from a single account and a single shared backend across web and mobile.

Reviews & ratings

How a place's score is built

Exceptional 22%
Wonderful 31%
Very good 27%
Pleasant 14%
Fair 6%
1

Star rating + comment

Authenticated users leave 1–5 stars with optional photos. One review per user per place.

2

Helpful & flags

Others mark reviews as helpful or report them for moderation.

The calculation
avgRating = Σ (rating) review count
5 4 3
= (5 + 4 + 3) ÷ 3 = 4.0
3

Recalculated on every change

A server-side aggregation — $avg over non-deleted reviews — updates the place's avgRating & reviewCount whenever a review is added, edited or removed. Deleted reviews reset it to 0.
